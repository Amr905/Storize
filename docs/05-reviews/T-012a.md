### Adversarial probes
- **Atomicity (drift):** the skew check is in the same `transaction()` as the read and the write (`GovernorLeaseDao.acquire`). Not one conditional UPDATE, but the connection is shared, so transactions serialise. Test: 4 concurrent `acquire` calls (holders B, C, B, C) from 2 repos on one database after a 3 h back-jump gave exactly one holder; a race on a fresh row also gave one holder.
- **Forward jump:** lease blocks at 9:59, frees at 10:00, frees after +30 d.
- **Old holder after a skewed takeover:** `renew` and `writeBucket` return false, so it cannot clobber the new holder.
- **Inherited state:** the takeover keeps the stored bucket including a 20-day `backoffUntil` and x4. The governor then refuses with `GovernorPaused` whose `retryAfter` is at most 24 h (the T-012 round-2 clamp holds).
- **Retry, partial success (write lands, then throws):** 5 min, 15 min default and 1 s pauses stay at x2 with 1 and 2 landed-then-thrown writes. The `_withBackoff` "already paused" guard makes the retry idempotent. Escalation to x4 is NOT possible in these cases. Exception: see F-1.
- **Lease state during retries:** the holder keeps the lease (held at both retry waits) and no other governor can slip in. After a retried record another governor is refused with `GovernorPaused`.
- **Guardrails:** `git diff` shows port signature changes: none (docs only in `governor_lease_repo.dart`); schema: no change (`tables.dart`, `.g.dart` untouched); no secrets in the diff; no Instagram imports in domain; the governor is not bypassed. gitleaks is not installed here; CI is the gate.

### Findings
| ID | Severity | Finding | Recommendation |
|----|----------|---------|----------------|
| F-1 | Low | If the limit write lands and then throws, and the `retryAfter` is shorter than the 200 ms retry delay (for example 100 ms), the pause has expired when the retry runs, so the same limit is counted again and the multiplier goes x2 to x4. Pinned by the test "KNOWN EDGE". Real impact is negligible: `Retry-After` is in whole seconds and `defaultRetryAfter` is 15 min. A drift write that commits and then throws is itself rare. | Accept, or enforce a minimum pause. If the test starts failing because the behaviour was fixed, flip its expectation to 2. |
| N-1 | Note | ttl mismatch: a taker with a shorter ttl treats a longer lease as expired (30 min lease taken by a 10 min taker); a longer-ttl taker is still blocked. `leaseTtl` is one value in `GovernorConfig` (default 10 min), so a mismatch only happens across a config hotfix and is harmless. | Add one line to the `GovernorLeaseRepo` doc: all holders of an account must use the same ttl. |
| N-2 | Note | The skew rule applies to `acquire` only. `renew`/`writeBucket` use `_held`, which is correct because they check the caller's own holder. | None. |
